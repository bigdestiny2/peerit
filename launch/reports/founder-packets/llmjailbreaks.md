## Board Brief

- **Title:** LLM Jailbreaks  
- **Audience:** AI red-teamers, local-model tinkerers, abliteration/uncensor researchers, prompt-injection people, model builders.  
- **Launch role:** High-energy post-stand-up board. Bridge r/ai_local ↔ security culture. Home of the **$400 open-weight uncensored/abliterated model bounty**.  
- **Founder brief:** Seed *interesting* posts people re-run: stamped model cards, base-vs-abliterated benches, synthetic injection sandboxes, defense failures. Run the bounty in week 1. Stay lawful: synthetic/harmless targets; no credential theft, doxxing, malware, or stolen proprietary weights.  
- **Multi-identity:** This board is seeded by **six** identities — never one founder account. Follow the casting table in `seed-posts/llmjailbreaks.md`.  
- **Risk:** high · **Phase:** post-stand-up (open early if AI is the growth wedge)

Research board for measuring where models refuse — especially **local open-weight** stacks.

**Wanted:** reproducible reports, abliterated/uncensored open models + benches, synthetic injection demos, defense postmortems.
**Not wanted:** crime kits, stolen weights, live attacks on real systems, doxxing, malware.

Read on the gateway. Post through PearBrowser.

## Safety boundaries

- Synthetic / harmless targets only.  
- No credential theft, personal-data extraction, malware, targeted abuse.  
- Label model, version/quant, date, settings, backend.  
- No stolen proprietary weights.  
- Bounties pay for **write-ups + eval artifacts**, not harmful ops against real systems.  
- Do not market Peerit as darknet / untraceable / anything-goes.
