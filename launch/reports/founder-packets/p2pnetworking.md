Sources: `launch/communities.json` (b21d5b8605f6), `docs/GROWTH_AUTOMATION_SPEC.md` (f56ae9fe0dee)

## Board Brief

- Title: P2P Networking
- Audience: Protocol engineers and operators working on discovery, NAT traversal, replication, relays, and peer-to-peer performance.
- Launch role: Deep technical companion to P2P Builders and a home for protocol-level discussion.
- Founder brief: Seed packet traces, architecture comparisons, benchmarks, failure reports, and small reproducible experiments across Hypercore, libp2p, WebRTC, BitTorrent, IPFS, and related systems.
- Moderation risk: low
- Activation phase: post-stand-up

## Deliverables

- Create one owner/moderator identity for r/p2pnetworking.
- Publish 16 quality starter posts before paid traffic.
- Publish at least 4 open-ended discussion prompts.
- Pin one norms post and exercise the moderation overlay before launch.
- Reply daily during the first launch week so the board does not feel empty.

## Starter Prompt Checklist

- "What is one P2P Networking workflow that would benefit from no central account or server?"
- "Show a concrete P2P Networking build, benchmark, migration, or failure report."
- "What should r/p2pnetworking moderate quickly during launch week?"
- "Where does Peerit's P2P model fit this audience, and where is it still rough?"

## Board-Specific Seed Themes

- NAT traversal techniques that still fail in ordinary home networks
- Relay versus direct-connect latency and availability measurements
- Discovery, replication, and identity trade-offs across P2P stacks
- Postmortems from real peer-to-peer network failures
