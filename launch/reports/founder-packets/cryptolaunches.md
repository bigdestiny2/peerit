# Founder Packet: r/cryptolaunches

Sources: `launch/communities.json` (b21d5b8605f6), `docs/GROWTH_AUTOMATION_SPEC.md` (f56ae9fe0dee)

## Board Brief

- Title: Crypto Launches
- Audience: Crypto-native users who want sharp launch banter, launch tracking, due diligence, and post-launch accountability.
- Launch role: Fast-moving culture board with strong reply potential and a natural anti-scam angle.
- Founder brief: Seed candid launch reviews, disclosed founder AMAs, token-mechanics breakdowns, prediction threads, and postmortems. Ban undisclosed paid promotion, impersonation, coordinated pumping, fake urgency, and guaranteed-return claims.
- Moderation risk: high
- Activation phase: post-stand-up

## Deliverables

- Create one owner/moderator identity for r/cryptolaunches.
- Publish 16 quality starter posts before paid traffic.
- Publish at least 5 open-ended discussion prompts.
- Pin one norms post and exercise the moderation overlay before launch.
- Reply daily during the first launch week so the board does not feel empty.

## Pinned Norms Post Template

```markdown
# Welcome to r/cryptolaunches

This board is for Crypto-native users who want sharp launch banter, launch tracking, due diligence, and post-launch accountability.

Bring concrete links, demos, build notes, questions, and careful comparisons.
Keep claims precise: Peerit has no central account, uses signed identities, and gates posting with small proof-of-work.
Do not position this board around illegal content, harassment, doxxing, ban evasion, or "anything goes" moderation.

Read on the gateway. Post through PearBrowser.
```

## Safety Boundaries

- Require disclosure of paid relationships, allocations, and material holdings.
- No coordinated pumping, impersonation, fake scarcity, or guaranteed-return language.
- Keep criticism evidence-led while allowing jokes and sharp banter.
- Do not say: Anything goes.
- Do not say: Darknet Reddit.
- Do not say: No moderation.
- Do not say: Untraceable.
- Do not say: Use this to evade bans.
- Do not advertise into darknet-market communities.
- Do not position Peerit as a place for illegal content.
- Do not target hate, harassment, doxxing, exploitation, or violent-extremism communities.
