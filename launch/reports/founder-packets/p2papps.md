# Founder Packet: r/p2papps

Sources: `launch/communities.json` (b21d5b8605f6), `docs/GROWTH_AUTOMATION_SPEC.md` (f56ae9fe0dee)

## Board Brief

- Title: P2P Apps
- Audience: People building, testing, reviewing, and using peer-to-peer applications.
- Launch role: Accessible product board that turns protocol interest into apps people can try.
- Founder brief: Seed hands-on app reviews, demos, UX critiques, interoperability ideas, and requests for testers. Require a working link or concrete build detail for launch posts.
- Moderation risk: low
- Activation phase: post-stand-up

## Deliverables

- Create one owner/moderator identity for r/p2papps.
- Publish 14 quality starter posts before paid traffic.
- Publish at least 4 open-ended discussion prompts.
- Pin one norms post and exercise the moderation overlay before launch.
- Reply daily during the first launch week so the board does not feel empty.

## Pinned Norms Post Template

```markdown
# Welcome to r/p2papps

This board is for People building, testing, reviewing, and using peer-to-peer applications.

Bring concrete links, demos, build notes, questions, and careful comparisons.
Keep claims precise: Peerit has no central account, uses signed identities, and gates posting with small proof-of-work.
Do not position this board around illegal content, harassment, doxxing, ban evasion, or "anything goes" moderation.

Read on the gateway. Post through PearBrowser.
```

## Starter Prompt Checklist

- "What is one P2P Apps workflow that would benefit from no central account or server?"
- "Show a concrete P2P Apps build, benchmark, migration, or failure report."
- "What should r/p2papps moderate quickly during launch week?"
- "Where does Peerit's P2P model fit this audience, and where is it still rough?"

## Board-Specific Seed Themes

- A weekly roundup of P2P apps people can actually try
- Honest onboarding reviews from first-time users
- Build logs showing offline, sync, or recovery behavior
- Interoperability wishes between independent P2P apps
