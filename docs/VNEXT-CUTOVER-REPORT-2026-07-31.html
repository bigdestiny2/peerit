02What was achieved

The substrate, live and self-maintaining

  • Two blind relays in production (Sydney + Dallas, p2phiverelay.xyz): blind daemon + blind edge serving signed opaque cells. They cannot read, index, or forge application content — proven by boundary captures showing zero app vocabulary in relay-visible bytes.
  • Descriptor rotation automated — hourly, on both boxes, fail-closed by construction. The recurring ~24 h expiry cliffs that caused three outages are eliminated as a class.
  • Admission v5 landed — pricing matches what the wire advertises: sizeClass-2 cells (the bounty post was previously unpriceable) and lease classes 1 / 7 / 30 / 90 days. The daily-expiry trap is dead at the policy level.
  • Operations hardened — a 100 %-full disk on syd (47 GB of legacy hypercore accumulation) was archived byte-verified and cleared to 24 %; the hourly rotation, admission, and storage budgets are now evidence-logged fleet facts, not tribal knowledge.

The launch seed, live and honest

  • 34 records on both relays: 11 community claims (r/ai_local + r/p2pbuilders with content; nostr, selfhosted, privacy, homelab, localfirst, hiverelay, fediverse, cypherpunk, osint as ready containers), 17 posts, 6 replies — published as opaque cells with per-relay signed receipts and byte-exact readback on every record.
  • 10 minted personas, one keypair each, aged per plan; OP-share caps held on both boards; every claim in the corpus grounded in real benchmarks or verified source — the one overclaim found ("atomic quorum") was rewritten to the literal truth before publish.
  • A $200 launch bounty, paid in BTC, with concrete anti-scam mechanics: 5 × $40 to the winning board's top five distinct contributors, public adjudication with recomputable metrics, 48 h comment window, signed claims, and a hash-committed seed-key list that proves no house key can win.
  • 90-day leases on all seed cells, with an automated renewal checkpoint and a bootstrap-expiry checkpoint scheduled ahead of it — nothing lapses silently.

The browser breakthrough

  • A stock Chrome is now a zero-trust client of the blind substrate. Fresh browser + signed bootstrap + relay URLs → descriptor chains verified to pinned genesis → all communities enumerated → feeds assembled — 40 verified CELL.GETs, 0 PUTs, byte-exact, on Chromium 145 / 149 / 151 identically.
  • Under a strict production CSP (script-src 'self'), zero violations, zero page errors — the environment real users actually have, not a lab browser.
  • Found and fixed properly, not around: the vendored crypto eagerly compiled WebAssembly at import (a hard CompileError under CSP). The defect was latent in an accepted bundle because every prior proof ran in Node. The fix kept the CSP intact and removed the WASM packages from the build — pure-JS fallbacks, protocol byte-identical across old-WASM Node, pure-JS Node, and pure-JS Chromium.
  • Signed seed bootstrap as cold-start discovery: the reader needs no account and no prior state. A signed artifact names the live cells per relay with capabilities; the client verifies it against a pinned discovery authority and recovers everything GET-only.

03What the vNext cutover means

The meaning is architectural, and it is precise — not slogans:

  • The relay became a utility, not an authority. HiveRelay stores and forwards signed opaque cells. It can withhold data but never forge it. Availability is its only job; authenticity belongs to signatures, verified by every client.
  • App-agnostic substrate, for real. The same blind fabric now carries Peerit's records and can carry any other app's — the Ultimate Sports engine already builds on the same primitives. The relay never learns which app a cell belongs to.
  • The dual-ship story is honest at last. "Same signed data, native P2P or a normal browser" was aspirational for the blind path until the CSP-safe rebuild made the normal browser actually work. Now it is demonstrated: native PearBrowser is swarm; a stock browser is a zero-trust client. One protocol, two transports, no fork.
  • The transport barrier was not broken — it was made irrelevant for integrity. A browser still cannot open raw sockets or join a swarm. What changed: it no longer needs to. Because authenticity is math, not transport, a browser gets P2P-grade integrity over plain HTTPS. That is the exact scope of the breakthrough — and it is not claimed as more.
  • No fake claims, as a method. Every statement in the seed and in this launch is either drill-proven on the live fleet or labelled as deferred. Four separate times, the chain stopped instead of shipping something that would pass CI but fail in reality — the atomic-ingress deployability gap, the probe-purge ambiguity, the inert seq-20 candidate, and the WASM/CSP browser defect. That discipline is why the result can be trusted.

06Honest boundaries

  • Claim boundary: LIVE_PUBLIC_TEST_ONLY. Two owner-operated relays, not GA, not independent operators.
  • Proven: blind storage + transport on the live relays; publisher writes with receipts and byte-exact readback; browser cold-recovery of all seed content under strict CSP; automated rotation; priced long leases; honest bounty mechanics.
  • Not yet proven / deferred: INBOX/CORE composition; public browser-based posting; independent operators; the GA gate (22 blockers disclosed-open); the seq-19 accepted bundle carries the now-disclosed WASM/CSP defect and is queued for reassessment.
  • The one-line story, calibrated: "Stock browsers are zero-trust clients of the blind substrate — they verify everything and trust no server." True and proven. Not claimed: "we broke the browser transport barrier" — that stands, and beating it is the WebRTC/WSS-bridge work ahead.
