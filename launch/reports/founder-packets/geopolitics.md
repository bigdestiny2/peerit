# Founder Packet: r/geopolitics

Sources: `launch/communities.json` (b21d5b8605f6), `docs/GROWTH_AUTOMATION_SPEC.md` (f56ae9fe0dee)

## Board Brief

- Title: Geopolitics
- Audience: People following diplomacy, conflict, trade, sanctions, elections, and shifting alliances.
- Launch role: Post-stand-up current-affairs board with a reason to return every day.
- Founder brief: Seed source-led news threads, competing interpretations, maps, and forecasts. Clearly separate reporting from opinion; remove doxxing, dehumanization, unverified atrocity claims, and operational targeting information.
- Moderation risk: high
- Activation phase: post-stand-up

## Deliverables

- Create one owner/moderator identity for r/geopolitics.
- Publish 16 quality starter posts before paid traffic.
- Publish at least 5 open-ended discussion prompts.
- Pin one norms post and exercise the moderation overlay before launch.
- Reply daily during the first launch week so the board does not feel empty.

## Pinned Norms Post Template

```markdown
# Welcome to r/geopolitics

This board is for People following diplomacy, conflict, trade, sanctions, elections, and shifting alliances.

Bring concrete links, demos, build notes, questions, and careful comparisons.
Keep claims precise: Peerit has no central account, uses signed identities, and gates posting with small proof-of-work.
Do not position this board around illegal content, harassment, doxxing, ban evasion, or "anything goes" moderation.

Read on the gateway. Post through PearBrowser.
```

## Starter Prompt Checklist

- "What is one Geopolitics workflow that would benefit from no central account or server?"
- "Show a concrete Geopolitics build, benchmark, migration, or failure report."
- "What should r/geopolitics moderate quickly during launch week?"
- "Where does Peerit's P2P model fit this audience, and where is it still rough?"

## Board-Specific Seed Themes

- A daily sourced briefing with links from more than one region
- A steelman-both-sides analysis of a current diplomatic dispute
- A prediction thread with dated claims that can be scored later
- A map or primary-source explainer with its limitations stated

## Safety Boundaries

- Label analysis, allegation, and confirmed reporting distinctly.
- No doxxing, dehumanization, terror propaganda, or real-time targeting information.
- Require a source for factual claims about breaking events.
- Do not say: Anything goes.
- Do not say: Darknet Reddit.
- Do not say: No moderation.
- Do not say: Untraceable.
- Do not say: Use this to evade bans.
- Do not advertise into darknet-market communities.
- Do not position Peerit as a place for illegal content.
- Do not target hate, harassment, doxxing, exploitation, or violent-extremism communities.
