# Founder Packet: r/gambling

Sources: `launch/communities.json` (b21d5b8605f6), `docs/GROWTH_AUTOMATION_SPEC.md` (f56ae9fe0dee)

## Board Brief

- Title: Gambling & Betting
- Audience: Adults discussing betting culture, odds, launches, promotions, regulation, and harm reduction.
- Launch role: High-engagement post-stand-up board with explicit age, disclosure, and jurisdiction controls.
- Founder brief: Seed odds analysis, product reviews, disclosed and jurisdiction-labelled promotions, industry news, and harm-reduction discussion. No minors, unlicensed offers, misleading bonuses, guaranteed wins, deposit pressure, or attempts to bypass self-exclusion.
- Moderation risk: high
- Activation phase: post-stand-up

## Deliverables

- Create one owner/moderator identity for r/gambling.
- Publish 12 quality starter posts before paid traffic.
- Publish at least 4 open-ended discussion prompts.
- Pin one norms post and exercise the moderation overlay before launch.
- Reply daily during the first launch week so the board does not feel empty.

## Safety Boundaries

- Adults only; label every promotion by jurisdiction and commercial relationship.
- No unlicensed offers, misleading bonuses, guaranteed wins, or deposit pressure.
- Never encourage bypassing limits, self-exclusion, identity checks, or local law.
- Do not say: Anything goes.
- Do not say: Darknet Reddit.
- Do not say: No moderation.
- Do not say: Untraceable.
- Do not say: Use this to evade bans.
- Do not advertise into darknet-market communities.
- Do not position Peerit as a place for illegal content.
- Do not target hate, harassment, doxxing, exploitation, or violent-extremism communities.
